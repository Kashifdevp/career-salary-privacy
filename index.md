# Privacy Policy for Career Salary Compare

Last updated: October 2026

Career Salary Compare ("the app") helps you compare career information and salary estimates.

## Information we collect
The app does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive your personal data.

## Data stored on your device
Your favorite careers and your theme choice are saved only on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Advertising
The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) you are asked for consent first and can change your choice in the app Settings. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Salary information
Salary figures in the app are estimates for general information only. They are not offers, guarantees or professional advice.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
